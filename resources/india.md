## Suggested path (example)

1. **Year 1:** GSSoC or SSoC + first merged PRs on a GSoC org you like  
2. **Year 2:** GSoC or Summer of Bitcoin + SIH or Devfolio hackathon for teams  
3. **Parallel:** C4GT or FOSSEE if gov-tech or science OSS fits your major  

See [choose-a-program.md](choose-a-program.md).
