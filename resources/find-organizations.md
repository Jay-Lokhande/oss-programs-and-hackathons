# Find organizations to contribute to

Programs are the door; **organizations** are who you actually work with.

Full org browser notes: [../organizations/README.md](../organizations/README.md).

## High-signal org families (examples)

- **Bitcoin:** Core-adjacent tooling, wallets, education (see Summer of Bitcoin project list each year)
- **Linux / infra:** Kernel, systemd, GNOME, KDE, CNCF projects (often LFX + GSoC)
- **Science / India:** FOSSEE, eSim, Spoken Tutorial ecosystem
- **Web / app:** Mozilla, Wikimedia, Django, Ruby, Rails (check each org’s issue tracker)

## What to do before you apply

1. Read `CONTRIBUTING.md` or `README` in the org repo.
2. Fix one small issue and get a review.
3. Introduce yourself on their **official** chat/list (not cold email unless they ask for it).

See [how-to-apply.md](how-to-apply.md).
