# Hacktoberfest

**What it is:** DigitalOcean’s annual **October** event — typically **4 accepted PRs** in the month for badges and swag (rules change; read the current year).

**Not a substitute for:** Paid programs like GSoC or Outreachy. Use it as a **starting point**, not your only OSS activity.

**Official:** [hacktoberfest.com](https://hacktoberfest.com/)
