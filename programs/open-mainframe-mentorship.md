# Open Mainframe Project Mentorship

**What it is:** Mentorship around **Linux on mainframe**, Z ecosystems, and related open projects under the Open Mainframe Project.

**Who can apply:** Varies by mentorship call — often students and career switchers with systems interest.

**Stipend:** Varies by project (check each cohort).

**Official:** [openmainframeproject.org](https://openmainframeproject.org/)

**Related:** Many mainframe projects also appear under **LFX Mentorship**.
