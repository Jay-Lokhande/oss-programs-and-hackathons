# FOSSASIA Codeheat

**What it is:** FOSSASIA’s rolling **coding contest** — contributors earn points for merged work on FOSSASIA projects (not a multi-month stipend internship).

**Who can apply:** Open to contributors worldwide.

**Rewards:** Prizes and recognition (verify current Codeheat rules).

**Official:** [codeheat.org](https://codeheat.org/) · [fossasia.org](https://fossasia.org/)

**Tip:** FOSSASIA is also a long-time **GSoC** organization — see [find-organizations.md](../resources/find-organizations.md).
