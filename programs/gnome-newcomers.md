# GNOME Newcomers / internships

**What it is:** **GNOME** desktop and app stack — internships and Outreachy/GSoC projects rather than a single “GNOME Summer” brand (see also Season of KDE-style programs elsewhere).

**Who can apply:** Via **GSoC**, **Outreachy**, or GNOME job/intern postings when listed.

**Stipend:** GSoC/Outreachy stipends when participating through those programs.

**Official:** [welcome.gnome.org](https://welcome.gnome.org/) · [wiki.gnome.org](https://wiki.gnome.org/)
