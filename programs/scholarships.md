# Scholarships & fellowships (global)

## University-specific (search pattern)

Most top universities list **merit / need-based aid** on their own `.edu` domains — this repo does not duplicate those lists. Use [classcentral.com](https://www.classcentral.com/) or each university’s **Financial Aid** page.
