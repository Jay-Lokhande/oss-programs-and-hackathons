# eSim Summer Internship (FOSSEE)

**What it is:** FOSSEE **eSim** (electronic simulation) internship for students contributing to the open eSim ecosystem.

**Who can apply:** Primarily Indian students — verify FOSSEE announcements.

**Stipend:** Paid when the summer internship cycle runs.

**Official:** [esim.fossee.in](https://esim.fossee.in/)

**Related:** [fossee-summer-fellowship.md](fossee-summer-fellowship.md)
