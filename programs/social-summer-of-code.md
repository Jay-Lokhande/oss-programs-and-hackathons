# Social Summer of Code (SSoC)

**What it is:** A **~2-month** India-led open-source program (large contributor community). Contributors join projects, merge PRs, and earn points on a leaderboard — certificates, prizes, and swag rather than a GSoC-scale cash stipend.

**Who can apply:** Students and developers worldwide; beginner-friendly tracks exist.

**Typical timeline (verify each season on the portal):**

- Registration — often **April–May**
- Coding phase — often **June–August**
- Results — often mid-August

**Cost:** Free to register.

**Official:** [socialsummerofcode.com](https://socialsummerofcode.com/) · Portal: [portal.socialsummerofcode.com](https://portal.socialsummerofcode.com/)

**Security:** Only trust contact from **socialsummerofcode@gmail.com** and verified social accounts listed on the official site.

**Tip:** Good ramp before GSoC/Outreachy; pair with paid programs once you have merged PRs to show.
