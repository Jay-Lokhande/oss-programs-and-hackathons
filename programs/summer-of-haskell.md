# Summer of Haskell

**What it is:** Haskell.org’s hub for **Google Summer of Code** projects in the Haskell ecosystem — ideas, mentors, and proposal tips when Haskell.org is a GSoC mentoring organization.

**Who can apply:** Same as GSoC (18+, contributor rules on the official GSoC site) with interest in Haskell (GHC, libraries, tools).

**Stipend:** GSoC stipend (location-based) when participating through Google’s program.

**Official:** [summer.haskell.org](https://summer.haskell.org/) · Ideas list updated each GSoC cycle

**Note:** This is not a separate employer — apply through **GSoC** with Haskell.org as the org.
