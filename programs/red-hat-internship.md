# Red Hat Open Source Internship

**What it is:** Internships working on **Fedora, RHEL ecosystem, middleware, and upstream** projects Red Hat contributes to.

**Who can apply:** Students and grads — listings on Red Hat jobs portal when open.

**Stipend:** Paid employment/internship wages (region-specific).

**Official:** [redhat.com/en/jobs/internships](https://www.redhat.com/en/jobs/internships)
