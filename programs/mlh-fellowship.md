# MLH Fellowship

**What it is:** A fellowship program (not identical to GSoC) with tracks such as **Open Source**, **Software Engineering**, and **Partners** fellowships. Structure and pay depend on the track and season.

**Who can apply:** Primarily students / early-career developers — check current eligibility on the MLH site.

**Official:** [fellowship.mlh.io](https://fellowship.mlh.io/)

**Note:** Compare stipend, time commitment, and whether the track is open-source-focused vs industry partner work before applying.
