# Eclipse Foundation Mentorship

**What it is:** Mentored contributions to **Eclipse IDE, Jakarta EE, IoT, and foundation projects** — often coordinated with Outreachy, LFX, or GSoC rather than a single “Eclipse Summer” brand.

**Who can apply:** Check the specific project’s call (many use GSoC/LFX).

**Official:** [eclipse.org](https://www.eclipse.org/) · [Eclipse GSoC](https://wiki.eclipse.org/Google_Summer_of_Code)
