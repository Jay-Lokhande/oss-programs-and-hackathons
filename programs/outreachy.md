# Outreachy

**What it is:** Paid, remote **3-month** internships with free/open-source communities. Projects span engineering, UX, documentation, data science, community, and more.

**Who can apply:** People **underrepresented in tech** (see Outreachy’s eligibility page for the current definition and rules).

**Stipend:** **$7,000 USD** total for the internship (check official terms each round).

**Rounds:** Typically **May–August** and **December–March** cohorts.

**Process (high level):**

1. **Initial application** — often due ~2 weeks after applications open
2. Contribute to projects during the contribution period
3. **Final application** with specific project(s)

**Important:** The project list may be finalized **after** the initial deadline — Outreachy asks you to apply even if you do not yet see a perfect project.

**Official:** [outreachy.org](https://www.outreachy.org/) · [Applicant guide](https://www.outreachy.org/applicants/)
