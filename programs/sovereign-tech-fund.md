# Sovereign Tech Fund (STF) — funding

**What it is:** German **funding** for maintainability of critical open-source infrastructure (grants, not a student internship calendar).

**Who can apply:** Maintainers and organizations with concrete proposals.

**Official:** [sovereign.tech](https://www.sovereign.tech/)

**Tip:** Pair with NLnet/EU grants if you are building OSS infra, not for first-time student stipends.
