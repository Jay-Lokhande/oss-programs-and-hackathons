# Summer of Bitcoin

**What it is:** A global, remote, mentored summer program for **university students** to work on real Bitcoin open-source projects.

**Tracks:**

- **Developer** — protocol-adjacent tooling, infrastructure, applications
- **Designer** — UX, product design, interfaces for Bitcoin software

**Who can apply:** University students, 18+. Selection emphasizes **proof of work** (OSS contributions, shipped tools, workshops).

**Duration:** ~12 weeks (two cohorts per year in some cycles — check official announcements).

**Stipend:** Paid in **Bitcoin (BTC)** by default; PPP-based by location (often cited around **~$3,000–$6,600 USD equivalent** — use the official stipend table). Paid in installments after evaluations.

**Typical application window:** Often opens **February**, deadline mid-February (verify each year).

**Official:** [summerofbitcoin.org](https://www.summerofbitcoin.org/) · Student guide: [guide.summerofbitcoin.org](https://guide.summerofbitcoin.org/)

**Community:** Strong alumni networks (e.g. Bitshala in India) — search social for “Summer of Bitcoin” + your region.
