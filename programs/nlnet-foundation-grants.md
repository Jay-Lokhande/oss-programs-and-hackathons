# NLnet Foundation (grants)

**What it is:** European foundation funding **privacy, trust, and open internet** tech — grants for individuals and teams to build FOSS (not identical to a student internship, but real funding for OSS work).

**Who can apply:** Project leads and developers with a concrete proposal (students sometimes apply with mentor support).

**Stipend / funding:** Project-based grants (amount varies).

**Official:** [nlnet.nl](https://nlnet.nl/) · [NGI Zero commons fund](https://nlnet.nl/commonsfund/)

**Tip:** Pair with ESoC or GSoC if you want mentorship **and** later funding for the same idea.
