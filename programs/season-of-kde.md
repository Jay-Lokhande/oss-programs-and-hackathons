# Season of KDE

**What it is:** KDE’s seasonal program for students and contributors to work on KDE desktop and application ecosystem projects with mentorship.

**Stipend:** Certificates and swag (not a cash stipend like GSoC — verify current benefits).

**Typical applications:** Often open around **December** each year.

**Official:** [season.kde.org](https://season.kde.org/)

**Tip:** KDE uses Qt/C++; good if you care about Linux desktop, Plasma, and app development.
