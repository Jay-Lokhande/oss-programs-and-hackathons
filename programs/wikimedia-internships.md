# Wikimedia Technical Internships

**What it is:** Internships and outreach for **MediaWiki, Wikipedia infrastructure, and Wikimedia tooling**.

**Who can apply:** Varies — some roles via Outreachy, others via Wikimedia Foundation job board.

**Stipend:** Paid when listed as internships; Outreachy has separate stipend rules.

**Official:** [MediaWiki — Wikimedia technical internships](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Wikimedia_technical_internships)

**Events:** [Wikimedia Hackathon](https://www.mediawiki.org/wiki/Wikimedia_Hackathon) — see [hackathons/flagship-events.md](../hackathons/flagship-events.md).
