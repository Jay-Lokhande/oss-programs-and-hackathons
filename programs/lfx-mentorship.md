# LFX Mentorship (Linux Foundation)

**What it is:** Mentored projects across the Linux Foundation ecosystem (CNCF, kernel-related projects, and more). Shorter cycles than a full summer in some terms; paid mentorship stipends for accepted mentees.

**Who can apply:** Varies by project — read each mentorship’s requirements.

**Stipend:** Often **~$3,000–$6,600** depending on region and project (check each mentorship).

**Typical timeline:** Multiple terms per year; application windows often **July–August** (verify on site).

**Official:** [mentorship.lfx.linuxfoundation.org](https://mentorship.lfx.linuxfoundation.org/)

**Tip:** Great follow-on after you have a few OSS contributions; many projects align with cloud-native, security, and infrastructure stacks.
