# Google Solution Challenge (GDSC)

**What it is:** Annual **student team competition** using Google tech to solve SDG-aligned problems (via Google Developer Student Clubs).

**Who can apply:** GDSC members / university students per current rules.

**Rewards:** Prizes and recognition (not a summer stipend like GSoC).

**Official:** [GDSC Solution Challenge](https://developers.google.com/community/gdsc-solution-challenge)

**Tip:** Listed under [hackathons/company-techathons.md](../hackathons/company-techathons.md) — multi-month build, not a 48h hackathon.
